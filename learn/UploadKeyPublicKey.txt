

2. Upload the Public Key to Meta 
You must register your public key with your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). 
Open public.pem in a text editor and copy the entire block, including:
-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY----- and -----END PUBLIC KEY-----.
Upload via the WhatsApp Business Encryption Endpoint:
Endpoint: POST /v21.0/{PHONE_NUMBER_ID}/whatsapp_business_encryption
Body: business_public_key=<YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_STRING>
Note: If using Postman, select x-www-form-urlencoded
